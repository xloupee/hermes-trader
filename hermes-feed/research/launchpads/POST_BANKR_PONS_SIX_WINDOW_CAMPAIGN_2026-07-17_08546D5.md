# Post-Bankr/Pons six-window paper campaign (`08546d5`)

## Verdict

Six independent five-minute windows were collected on Robinhood Chain `4663` from the exact source commit `08546d508d2c379e495960f8dc1425d4b6c44e9e`. All activity was read-only and paper-only: no wallet access, signing, broadcasting, canary, execution, deployment, or server access occurred.

## Hood taxonomy caveat

The frozen report's Hood values (`confirmed=1`, `eligible=3`, `misses=3`) are internally inconsistent because the quote collector admitted one launch plus later buy and sell rows for the same token, while the observer intentionally claims launches only. The frozen readiness aggregation counted all three quote rows as launch eligibility and counted the buy/sell rows as misses.

A supplemental launch-only calculation gives one eligible launch and zero launch detector misses, excluding two non-launch rows. This correction was **not emitted by the frozen tuple and is not promotion evidence**. A separate window-A wrapped/migrated Hood trade remains unclassified and blocked pending focused research.
